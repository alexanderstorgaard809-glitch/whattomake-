3. Who receives the data

We use these service providers (processors) to run the demo:

  • Render Services, Inc. (USA) hosts the website.
  • OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) forwards your documents to an AI model. We instruct it to use only providers with zero data retention that do not use data for training.
  • The AI model provider selected by OpenRouter under those restrictions (currently Google's Gemini models) processes the request and returns the result.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for marketing.

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, this relies on the safeguards each provider offers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. That is why there is no cookie banner.

6. Automated processing

An AI model reads your documents and drafts a letter. Amounts, interest and legal thresholds are calculated by fixed rules in our code, not by the AI. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect is made about you. You decide whether to use the letter, and you can edit all details first.
