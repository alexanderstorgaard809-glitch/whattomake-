How the calculation works

When a business pays you late, the law adds two things on top of the invoice. Which rules apply usually depends on where you are based, unless your contract chose another law.

EU (Directive 2011/7/EU)

  • Interest: ECB reference rate + at least 8 percentage points a year
  • Fixed compensation: €40 per late invoice
  • Default payment term: 30 days if nothing was agreed
  • Some countries add more, for example France (+10)

UK (Late Payment Act 1998)

  • Interest: Bank of England base rate + 8 percentage points a year
  • Fixed compensation: £40 under £1,000, £70 up to £9,999.99, £100 above
  • The base rate is fixed per invoice by the half-year it fell due
  • You can also claim reasonable extra recovery costs

Is this legal advice?

No. The calculator uses the standard statutory rules and simple daily interest. Your contract, national variations or a court may lead to a different amount. Check before you claim.
