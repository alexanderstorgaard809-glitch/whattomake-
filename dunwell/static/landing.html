Questions

Is this legal advice? {{icon:caret-down}}

No. Dunwell is a self-help tool that prepares a letter from the documents you provide. Check the details before sending, and talk to a lawyer for large or complex cases.

Which countries does it cover? {{icon:caret-down}}

Businesses in the 27 EU countries and the United Kingdom. The statutory rules usually follow your country and the court route follows your client's country, unless your contract says otherwise.

What if my client is a consumer? {{icon:caret-down}}

Statutory late-payment interest and fixed compensation only apply between businesses. For private clients the letter demands the invoiced amount only.

What does it cost? {{icon:caret-down}}

The demo is free while we test it, with a small limit per hour.

Does Dunwell send the letter? {{icon:caret-down}}

No. You send it yourself, ideally by email and a trackable method, and keep a copy as evidence.
