The awkward part

Weeks of polite reminders, WhatsApp messages that never get read, and a client abroad who assumes you won't bother. A lawyer costs more than the invoice.

What most freelancers don't know

Businesses that pay late owe you statutory interest plus a fixed sum per invoice (€40 in the EU, £40–£100 in the UK). Spelling that out in one formal letter changes the conversation.

FAQ

Is this legal advice?

No. Claim Pilot is a self-help tool that prepares a letter based on the documents you provide. Check the details before you send anything, and talk to a lawyer if the case is complex or large.

Which countries does it cover?

Businesses in the 27 EU countries and the United Kingdom. The statutory rules usually follow your country, and the court route follows your client's country. Your contract can change that.

Does it work if my client is a consumer?

The statutory interest and fixed compensation only apply between businesses. For private clients the letter only demands the invoiced amount.

What does it cost?

The demo is free while we test it. It has a small usage limit per hour.

Will it send the letter for me?

No. You get the text to send yourself, ideally by email and a trackable method, and you keep a copy as evidence.
